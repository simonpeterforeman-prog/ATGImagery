    Consumer Spend Sentiment

    McKinsey ConsumerWise Global Sentiment Survey · Feb 2026 · n=4,008

    Key Findings

    Net discretionary spend intent is negative across all discretionary categories and declined quarter over quarter, consistent with a typical post-holiday slowdown. Consumers are planning to spend selectively rather than pulling back across the board.

    Essential Holding Steady

    • Fresh produce leads essentials — only 9% of consumers plan to spend less
    • Baby supplies and meat/dairy remain resilient with positive net intent
    • Pet food, gasoline, and center-store groceries show stable spending patterns
    • Net intent remained relatively flat compared with previous quarter

    Semi-Disc. Mixed Signals

    • Vitamins/supplements and personal care hold near neutral
    • Toys saw the largest pullback in semi-discretionary (net intent down QoQ)
    • Beauty products and household supplies remain relatively stable
    • Vehicles show slight improvement from Q4

    Discretionary Selective Spending

    • Pet care services is the only discretionary category with positive net intent
    • Home improvement is a bright spot — up 11 percentage points vs Q4 2025
    • Domestic flights up 5 points, with gains across travel-related categories
    • Apparel, footwear, and accessories see 35–45% of consumers planning to spend less
    • Decorations and furniture have the weakest intent (43–45% spending less)

    Implications for ATG Imagery

    • Essential category imagery should emphasize everyday frequency — these purchases are consistent
    • Semi-discretionary imagery should lean into value and necessity — consumers are justifying these spends
    • Discretionary imagery should highlight experiential and seasonal moments — consumers are choosing selectively when and where to splurge
    • Travel and home improvement are counter-trend bright spots worth leaning into for Q2
